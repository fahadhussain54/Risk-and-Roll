==================================================
              RISK AND ROLL - RULES               
==================================================
1. Objective: Race against your opponent to reach 
   a score of 100 points first!
2. Turn Flow: On your turn, roll the dice and add 
   the value to your current turn score. You can 
   choose to hold your points safely or roll again.
3. The Danger (1s): If you roll a 1, your turn score 
   wipes to 0 unless you have an active Shield!
4. Power-Ups & Shop: Earn coins after every turn to 
   spend in the Item Shop on Shields, Rerolls, and 
   Double Points boosts.
5. Timed Mode: Make your decisions quickly under the 
   ticking clock or the game will default your choice!
==================================================
